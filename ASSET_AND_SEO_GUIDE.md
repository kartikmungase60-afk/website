## Replace homepage carousel images

Keep these exact filenames when uploading replacements:

- `public/assets/homepage/minecraft-hosting-hero.webp`
- `public/assets/homepage/hytale-hosting-hero.jpg`
- `public/assets/homepage/palworld-hosting-hero.jpg`
- `public/assets/homepage/vps-hosting-hero.webp`
- `public/assets/homepage/web-hosting-hero.webp`
- `public/assets/homepage/bot-hosting-hero.webp`
